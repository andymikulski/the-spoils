# Multi-Player Guidelines

Transcribed verbatim from the printed rules insert card
(`source/multiplayer_guidelines.jpg`). Printed typos are preserved
("ambigious"). Atom ids (`MP n`) are stable and are cited from `RULES.md`
and the rules test suite (`data/rule-tests/source/multiplayer.txt`).

## Verbatim text

> **Overview**
> The Spoils™ was designed as a two player game. However, we realize that the
> community enjoys occasionally playing with three or more players. These
> guidelines are meant to provide official answers to ambigious situations.
>
> **Defining Your Opponent**
> If a card refers to your opponent, or both players, select one of the other
> players as your opponent for the current block of rules text. Simply select
> a temporary opponent as needed, and repeat this whenever necessary.
>
> **House Rules**
> We encourage the use of house rules among your regular play group. There
> will invariably be combinations and effects that are too powerful in
> multi-player. Please establish a set of house rules to resolve situations
> that aren't fun for everyone.
>
> We also encourage the invention of new ways to play multi-player. If you
> find a good method, please share it with other play groups in the
> thespoils.com forums.
>
> *Questions? Check out the Comprehensive Rules at www.thespoils.com/rules,
> or ask about it in our forums at www.thespoils.com/forums.*

## Atoms

- **MP 1** — The game may be played with three or more players.
- **MP 2** — If a card refers to "your opponent", select one of the other
  players as your opponent for the current block of rules text.
- **MP 3** — If a card refers to "both players", the same selection applies:
  "both players" means you and the opponent selected for that block of rules
  text.
- **MP 4** — The selected opponent is temporary: it holds only for the
  current block of rules text, and a new selection is made each time one is
  needed.
- **MP 5** — House rules are encouraged for combinations that are too
  powerful in multi-player (non-normative; the engine exposes no house-rule
  hook for this — see `docs/house-rules-mods-design.md`).
