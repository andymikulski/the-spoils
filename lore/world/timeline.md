# Timeline

The cards never provide a calendar, so **there is no dated timeline**. This file groups
every statement about past events and orders them only where a card itself gives the order
("newly born", "150 years have passed", "in its last days", "one day"). Anything else is
listed under "Undated" with no placement implied.

Most of the history comes from a recurring in-world book, ***Marduun, a Forgotten History***,
credited once as "authored by the Right Honorable Basel E. Corgington, Esq" (*Adriel, Supreme
General of the Imperial Army*; *A Blazing Zero*). Cards quoting the book are marked 📖.

---

## 1. Before the war: the Marmothoan Empire and the Mau

- 📖 *Mau Caravan* (Seed Saga) — "In its last days, the Marmothoan Empire became deeply indebted to mixed-blood relatives of the Emperor - the Mau. Enraged and without suitable recourse, Padamose declared all debts to 'housecats' absolved, thereby forcing their immediate flee across the desert. Their destination? A small mining town on the coast called Clockman..."
- *Executive Order #6370u7* (Seed Saga) — "With Executive Order #6370u7, Padamose expelled the Mau from Marduun, clearing the way for a more sinister cult to form in their stead." (subtitle: "by yon decree, thou shalt not interrupt the time of pooping" ⚠)
- *Revivatate* (2nd Ed) — "In Clockman, they used to have a saying, 'Only two things are certain in life: death and taxes.' Then the Mau arrived."
- *Micromajig* (Ambassador Mode) — "the first 31f expatriates settled in Turncrank Gorge in the days of old Marmothoa."
- *Giada, the Elegant* (subtitle) — "last Sword to swear allegiance to Padamose"; flavor: "Giada's independence was matched only by her grit."
- *A Death Fit for a King* (subtitle) — "no emperor should outlive his empire"

## 2. The war for Marduun

Explicit sequencing from the book excerpts:

1. 📖 *A Blazing Zero* — "Battles raged long before war came to Marduun, but when war came, the sky stayed lit for half a century with brilliant blazes of might and destruction."
2. *Death of the Demon Knights* (subtitle) — "in a rage, Gideon killed them both"; 📖 flavor: "...what came next consumed the Empire and changed the world forever."
3. *Enter The Demon Knights* (subtitle) — "sick guitar solo goes here" ⚠ (flavor is sound effects).
4. 📖 *Mutilated* — "Elzibeth's madness knew no bounds. All became her victim, even her own son." (subtitle: "not coming back from that one")
5. 📖 *Invade!* — "In a fury, Adriel and the newly born Barduse descended on the capital for revenge."
6. 📖 *Storm the Gates* (subtitle: "this is how the Empire ends") — "In the end, it came down to Thabbashites versus Adriel and the newly formed Barduse versus Lingamites, Swords and the Seraphim. The winner? Don't be naïve."
7. 📖 *Runic Flee* — "During the final confrontation, Gloamspike emerged in a bid for power but, rejected by the masses, fled to Lugubria. The Gloamspike name, however, would not disappear from history; his sons would one day discover Voidal Humour ushering in the modern age of commerce and industry."
8. *Adriel, Supreme General of the Imperial Army* — 📖 "It is rumored that the former Great Sword Adriel, having forsaken king and realm, orchestrated the demise of his greatest ally Gideon and the rise of Emperor Barduse. It might not be an overestimation to say that the world we live in today is a direct result of his actions, although this author cannot be certain (for fear of getting blowed up)."

Other war-era statements (placement not stated):
- *Mud Prison* (subtitle) — "erected to hold in Gideon's sons"; flavor: "For their safety. And ours."
- *Lingamite Descendant* — "Yes, his grandfather was Gideon. His father was deaf and when all of Gideon's other children were turned into barduse, he remained unchanged. Through his sire, Gideon's legacy remains."
- *The Emperor Barduse* (Seed Saga) — subtitle "Gideon is gone. Only Barduse remains."; flavor: "Forged in a crucible of desperation and failure." (Seed 2 flavor: "There are only those with power, and those too weak to seek it.")
- *The Emperor Born* (subtitle) — "inside every Gideon..."; flavor: "...there's a demonic dragon-despot waiting to be let out."
- *Gideon's Legacy* (subtitle) — "terror and power runs in their blood"
- *Octo-Ba-Ba, the Devourer* — "Some historians trace Direstock's cannibalistic culture to Octo-Ba-Ba's influence."
- *Wasteland* — "The Blades escaped, just barely, but left behind a terrorscape filled with unspeakable horrors."
- *Zenobia's Intervention* — "Your destiny," Zenobia told them, "is to save this world from falling into total darkness like the Swords of Great Justice before." "Great Justice?" Pluck said. "More like Questionable Morality."

## 3. Aftermath: Marduun in ruins

- *Marduun Ruins* — "150 years have passed since Elzibeth and Gloamspike delivered Marduun into the hands of the Thabbashite hordes."
- *Theocratic Senate of Marduun*, *Marduun Baitings and Loan* — location cards still using the Marduun name (flavor: "We cannot speak for god / We just do the paperwork and take his bribes."; "Just sign here where it says we get to turn your corpse into a mindless zombie when you die."). The cards do not date these relative to the ruins.
- *Padamose Cultists* (Ungodly Mess) — "Clockman is home to all manner of cults. It didn't take long before Padamose found himself at the center of one."
- *Apparition Alley* — "Padamose wandered the streets of Clockman wondering how his beloved Marmothoa had given rise to such a vile and dangerous place."
- *Fade of the Devoured Emperor* (subtitle: "and now his watch had ended") — "With the god gone and his mission fulfilled, Padamose drifted to his final rest."
- *Shade of the Devoured Emperor* (subtitle) — "back on unfinished business" (card data: `padamose, emperor, mau`).

## 4. The era of Voidal Humour (commerce and industry)

- 📖 *Runic Flee* (above): Gloamspike's sons "would one day discover Voidal Humour ushering in the modern age of commerce and industry."
- *Unfortunate Fortune Hunter* — "Arcanist Lords amassed untold wealth as a result of the commodification of voidal humour. Untrusting of the banker Mau class and unable to protect his treasure in person, Gosche hid witch clocks throughout Goru-Goru Gidea to protect his riches."
- *Magic Vault* — "The vaults are a tremendous expenditure, true, but the interest they generate simply by storing the voidal humour in its materialized state will eventually pay for the investment many times over."
- *Voidal Humour Redistribution* — "One day, unless we find ourselves a source, Voidal Humour may run out. That's where the god comes in."
- *Voidal Bask* — "Living Gods are founts of Voidal Humour-- the raw stuff of magick."
- *Lugubrian Poacher* — "decades of voidal pollution"
- *Micromajig* — the earliest micromajig record "dates back only six years" (a death certificate of "Private Second Class Gozark"); "many anecdotal stories feature their appearance as much as a decade earlier."

## 5. The Living God episode

See `set-storylines.md` for the full card-by-card reading. In brief, the cards state:
- a Living God (*Zigalburgonglgr, the Stolen God*) is stolen/fought over by the Expedition (Lugubrian Council), the Unlikely Heroes, the Imperial Army and the Thabbashites;
- the god is to be delivered to Eldridge Gosche (*Retreat to Mount Mulrich*);
- *Theological Theft* — "Eldridge's therapist suggested he take something for his kleptomania, so he took a god."
