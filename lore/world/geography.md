# Geography

Every place name appearing on a card, with exactly what the cards say about it. No maps
exist; **relative positions are given only where a card states them**. Where a name appears
in several spellings it is recorded as printed.

Quote format: *Card Name* — "flavor" or (subtitle) "text".

---

## Large-scale names

### Luridia
Used as an all-encompassing place/world name; no card defines its extent.
- *Billowing Bouffant* — "Travel by air in Luridia is not advised."
- *Thabbashite Organist* — "Nature in Luridia is not without its bizarre tendencies evidenced in this case by the instrumental biology of this unmoving creature."
- *Chosen Reaper of Thabbash* — "Thabbashite society is largely a mystery to the peoples of Luridia, but one thing is certain: if you see a Reaper, run."
- *Micromajig* — the micromajig is "the single most common piece of voidal engineering in Luridia today."
- *Quotidian Concealer* — "Being able to actually see inside people was a massive step forward in Luridian medicine - and Imperical torture."
- *Key to the City* (subtitle) — "luridia is safe... for now"
- Adjective forms seen: "Luridian", "Luridia".

### Metatropea / "Metratropea"
A landmass (spelled both ways on cards) hosting campuses, foothills and an army sport league.
- *Omnidirectional Patrolmajig* — "The inventors of Acrolos have several campuses on mainland Metatropea. Security is of the highest concern."
- *Micromajig* (#MicromajigRevolution) — the hypothetical micromajig production complex is "hidden somewhere in the Metratropean foothills, is a shrine of the forbidden in Acrolosian culture."
- *Barbaric Rifleman* (2nd Ed) — "Every winter, the Imperial and Counter-Imperial armies compete against each other in the Metatropean Headball Championship Series. The amount of trash talk is unbelievable."

### Bodies of water
- **Specific Ocean** and **Nidian Sea** — *Fleet Majig*: "Acrolos is comprised of a chain of six islands on the border between the Specific Ocean and the Nidian Sea."
- **Ironic Ocean** and **Nidian Sea** — *Strategic Vision*: "And rule the waters from the Ironic Ocean to the Nidian Sea, free of the Countess at last!"

## Regions and nations

A blighted region associated with arcanists, the Void, and monsters.
- *Lugubrian Poacher* — "The wastelands of Lugubria were never hospitable, but decades of voidal pollution have made them host to many dangerous monsters. In a strange twist, the arcanists have begun to find uses for these beasts and will pay bounties to anyone foolish enough to pursue them. He who fights with monsters might take care lest he thereby become a monster."

## Cities and settlements

### Clockman
A coastal city; heavily commercial, criminal, cult-ridden, and a centre of Mau finance.
- *Mau Caravan* — the Mau's destination after fleeing across the desert: "A small mining town on the coast called Clockman..."
- *Revivatate* (2nd Ed) — "In Clockman, they used to have a saying, 'Only two things are certain in life: death and taxes.' Then the Mau arrived."
- *Padamose Cultists* — "Clockman is home to all manner of cults. It didn't take long before Padamose found himself at the center of one."
- *Goldmau Sacks Representative* — "Clockman's most powerful investment bank is a great vampire squid wrapped around the face of humanity, relentlessly jamming its blood funnel into anything that smells like money."
- *Pervasive Bodyguard* (2nd Ed) — "Since the Clockman Guard is infamously corrupt. Mau often feel the need to contract private security from their own kind to safeguard their personal interests."

- *Abandoned Testing Complex* — "Buried deep beneath layers of Clockman's history, it is not clear who had built it or why. The archives in Acrolos had no record of this place, but yet they found Kaiser prototypes, node terminals, all manner of the most advanced voidal technologies.
