## Living Gods (Thabbash)

- *The Primitive Tribes of Thabbash* (faction flavor) — "In the desert outside of Marduun, the warring Thabbash tribes worship their Living Gods. Incapable of communication and devoid of intelligence, The Living Gods melt people and animals, cause spontaneous combustion, and transform their followers into monsters. Their follower's only hope for survival will be the use of their own volition..."
- *Voidal Bask* — "Living Gods are founts of Voidal Humour-- the raw stuff of magick."
- *Chosen Reaper of Thabbash* — "Thabbashite society is largely a mystery to the peoples of Luridia, but one thing is certain: if you see a Reaper, run."
- *Thabbashite Priest of Zig* (subtitle) — "protector of the stolen god"; flavor "Not all Living Gods have priests, but all are as worshipped as they are misunderstood."
- *Personal God Shrine* (subtitle "unrequited reverence") — "At first, I found it curious the Thabbashites devoted themselves to worshipping beings that did not give them the least notice. Later, I realized this hardly distinguished them from any other cult."
- *Hasty Pursuit* (subtitle) — "thabbashites do not play around when you steal their gods"
- *Disposable Meatsack* — "The Thabbashites killed Imperial Army and Counter-Imperial Army nondiscriminately in defense of their god."

## Other religious figures and institutions (non-Thabbash)

- **Lingamites** — appear as a faction in the war for Marduun (*Storm the Gates*) and as a monastic order: *Lingamite Torture Monk* (Seed Saga subtitle) "we call this 'enhanced interrogation'"; *Lingamite Descendant* ("muh grad fodder wa a lingamite warror" / the Gideon lineage quote). See `../peoples/seraphim-and-dragons.md` / `../characters/marduun-era.md`.
- **Priest-type Banker cards** — *Swinish Elder* (subtitle "custodian of justice") "The smell of sin is such a...delicious...aroma."; *Purifying Priest* ("looks haunted to me. we'll just have to burn it." / "...and build a new church on the land. You know, to purify it. We'll be accepting donations in the fall."); *Property Condemner* ("haunted? it's my lucky day!" / "We thought the ghosts were sort of fun, but the priest kicked us out anyway. Something about the church taking care of it...."); *Dowsing Cleric* ("If you can think of a better way to find water, I'd looove to hear it."); *Temple Guardian* ("I thought 'arrow offering' day was next week..."); *Pilgrimage Auditor* ("oh, you want to rest at that shrine over there?" / "I'm sorry, but I'll have to deduct points for that."); *Fiscal Intercessor* ("blessed be the moolah" / "May this moolah begat more moolah…"). Card data: *Swinish Elder*, *Purifying Priest*, *Property Condemner*, *Dowsing Cleric*, *Temple Guardian* are `priest, mau`; *Pilgrimage Auditor* and *Fiscal Intercessor* are `mau` only. (*Thabbashite Deacon* is a Banker `priest` without `mau`.)
- *The Poshernacle* — "A large portable tent used for prayer, worship, and kicking back by the pool."
- *Cathédrale de Mau* — "money is god, greed is king, and corruption runs the rest" (subtitle).
- *Because God Said So!* (Seed/Seed Saga) — "thou shalt cast out yon lepers"; flavor lists punishments in mock-biblical diction ⚠.
- *Theocratic Senate of Marduun* — "Terrifying machine of state, as finite as it is deterministic." (Seed) / "We just do the paperwork and take his bribes." (Seed Saga, subtitle "we cannot speak for god")
- *Padamose Cultists* — "worshipping the undead" (subtitle).
- *Karmic Cake* — "An oversized reward for a small sacrifice".
- *Seraphim* are part of the war for Marduun (*Storm the Gates*) and of the Living God episode (*Wasted Wickedness*), but cards never call them gods; see `../peoples/seraphim-and-dragons.md`.
- *Zenobia* and other witches: `voidal-magic.md`.
- *God's Autograph* — "Can you carve your name into a stone twenty feet high? No? Then I guess you're not God." ⚠
- *Deicidal Detonation* — "BOOOOOOOM"; *Zenobian Mechanics Explained* — "i will tell you how to kill a god".
