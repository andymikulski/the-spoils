# Daily Life and Culture

Food, drink, sport, entertainment, books, language, medicine and everyday detail. Useful
as set-dressing for NPCs. Many entries are gags (⚠) but they are the only "ordinary life"
texture the catalog offers.

---

## Calendar and time
- *Arcanist's Almanac* — "Smarch 5th – Strong voidal storms from the southeast. Wear a bowler hat." (the only date on any card; "Smarch" is printed where a month name would go).
- Seasons: "winter" (*Barbaric Rifleman*: "Every winter…"; *Callow Liegeman*: "in the dead of winter"); *Stalwart Soldier*: "It was early winter when I went forth from my home base."
- Days: "Tuesday" (*Shrouded Demon*: "Except on Tuesdays, when it takes them bowling."); "Sunday" (*Doarden Flee Market*: "where traitors go on sale every Sunday"); "Saturday" (*Costly Filcher*); "Tuesday" (*Oversoon Lightfoot*: "the battle is next Tuesday").
- Ages: "6732 years young" (*Beefy Researcher* subtitle); "300 years in runecrafting school" (*Research Assistant*); "eighty years" for a dissertation (*Research Intern*); "a hundred years if he's a day" (*Runic Whale*).

## Food and drink
- **Mau**: catnip (*Armored Keep*, *Clandestine Resort*, *Club Mau Bouncer*'s Catnip Room), pudding (*Clandestine Resort* — "ah...at last! more pudding!"; *Vilghame Manewaft* — "He knows you ate his pudding." / "How can you have any pudding if you don't eat your meat?!"), feasts (*Clandestine Resort*; *Selective Gluttony* — "Only fifteen courses?! Outrage!").
- **Drinks**: wine ("Geldale" wine, *Balthassar Gristwalter*), rum (*Bottlebeard Brandyshanks*, *Derelict Seafarer*, *Lucky Bastard*), "Jinn of Elk" (*Card Counter*: "It must be all the Jinn of Elk he drinks."), milk (*Research Assistant* subtitle "don't drink the milk"), milkshake (*Micromajig Factory* "produces micromajigs and/or milkshakes").
- **Dishes named**: *Cowslaw* ("Also known as ruminant hash, or salmagundi stew."), *Clam Hash*, *Meat of the Mountain* (subtitle "with herb stuffed radicchio"), *Karmic Cake*, *Desserted Island*, *Hidden Sandwich* ("forged from the remains of lesser sandwiches" / "Never underestimate the deflective powers of pumpernickel."), *Melty Cheese*, *Spiky Pumpkin* ("first the slaughter. then the pie."), *Thabbashite Barbeque*, *12 Man Brunch of Doom* (a ritual meal), *Marmalade* (*Master Ritualist*), *Mustard* (*Odious Diva*), "soup du jour" (*Resurrection Technician*), "taco" (*12 Man Brunch of Doom* subtitle).
- **Cannibalism** is a stated practice in several places: *Direstock* (*Octo-Ba-Ba, the Devourer*), *Goru-Goru Gidea* ("bloodthirsty cannibals", *Eidolic Cove*), warlord troops (*Ruthless Platoon*, *Trophy Collector*, *Fleshripping Scenepuller*), Mau ("it's not a party until somebody gets devoured", *Mau Party*), and the *Balthassar Gristwalter* quote ("…you buy a manchild, and then you eat the manchild…"). Treat as set-dressing for brutal or satirical factions rather than a universal norm.
- **Foodstuffs/produce**: *Toxic Cultivator* — "People laugh at my toxic pusgourd. But did they slave for countless hours, rubbing it with fat and feeding it weasel paste?" (subtitle "this'll show those bastards at the fair!"); *Mushroom Merchant*; *Edible slimes* — *Puddle of Goo*.
- **Brands/products**: "Dentu-Grip" (*Prehensile Pirate*), "iDumb" (*iDumb De-Itemizer*: "we totally have this trademarked" ⚠), "Pwny Bomb", "Fat Pants", "Kitten Mittons" ("is your mau making too much noise all the time!?").

## Sport and spectacle
- **Metatropean Headball Championship Series** — played by the Imperial and Counter-Imperial armies each winter (*Barbaric Rifleman*, 2nd Ed).
- **Arena fighting** — *Arena Quarter-Finalist* ("Any rookie can swing a sword; mastery is about keeping your footing when the ring is thick with gore."), *Fallen Quarter-Finalist* (necromorph; a haunting poem about missing "the cheers, the blood and the spotlights"), *Reckless Challenger*, *Elbow Dropper* ("Illegal, Mean Jean!"), *Numba 1 Stunna!* ("prize fighter. literally."), *Rudo Mangod, Dragonweight Champion*, *Irresistible Bribe* ("Drugging the audience? … Bumping off last year's winner? … Buying the judge?"), *Executive Poaching* (golf commentary), *Single Combat* ("rudo vs. gor-blach").
- **Game tournaments of Acrolos** — "legendary in their frivolity and ferocity" (*Elite Team Registration*, 2nd Ed); *0p3r4710n 1337 H4x0rz* — "we are playing for the title of Ch4mp10n 0f teh Un1v3r53."
- **Conventions** — "6th Annual Deadly Objects Convention" (*Ambiguity Inhibitor*, 2nd Ed); *Hall of Conventions* (subtitle "let's not go there...it is a silly place").
- **Betting** — *War Racketeer* ("i'll take forty-over-thirty on the Living God getting blowed up!"), *Sly Bilker*, *Dauntless Gambler* ("i bluff your bluffing of my bluff"), *Hat-Sheep-Banana* (a game, *Lucky Charm*).
- **Carnival** — *Carnival King*, *Carnival Ringmaster*, *Carnival Whipmaster* ("the show in Acrolos was cancelled"), *Carnival Funhouse* ("Available for children's parties, bar mitzvahs, and funerals."), *Party Clowns*, *Dweedle Tee*, *Dazzling Trapezist*, *Titillating Contortionist*, *Hypnotic Illusionist*.
- **Street performance and nightlife** — *TZONE! Street Dancer*, *Motivational Dancer*, *Entrancing Harlot*, *Delusive Strumpet*, *Rosy Palmer, and Her Five Friends*, *Luxurious Gaming Establishment*, *Orgy Den Smoke Out*, *Club Mau Bouncer* ("there's no petting in the Catnip Room" / "None."), *Drainbow Crash* (subtitle "nightclub VIP"). Card subtype `dancer`, `performer`, `hedonist` are common in Rogue.
- **Fairs** — *Toxic Cultivator* (produce contest, above).

## Books, writing and communication

- ***Edible Slimes of Lugubria*** — by Meldring (*Puddle of Goo*).
- ***A Cancer in our Midst: On Sarcophelia and the Coming Plague*** — by Ernest Arndell (1st Ed) / "Ernest Amdell" (2nd Ed & Open Beta) (*Hyperphagic Sarcophyle*).
- **"the Style"** — *Shriever Attack*: "— Henry Raves, nailer of The Style" (1st and 2nd Ed).
- ***Arcanist's Almanac*** — weather/date notices (above).
- Library requests (*Cryptic Athenaeum*): "do you have 'Bartholomew and the Oobleck'...?" (Seed) and "do you have a book called 'How to Beat Barney?'" (Seed Saga).
- **Forms and orders**: *Form 37-Ohhhhh* and *Form 37-Q* (*Form 37-Ohhhhh*: "Not to be confused with form 37-Q."); *Executive Order #6370u7*; *Fat Pants*.
- **Language**: *Direspeak* (Direstock; *Micromajig* "the original Direspeak entry"); the elves' "unintelligible language" (*I Got the Gear!*: "Elves can construct any tool you need. However, their unintelligible language makes special orders particularly difficult."); the hamsters' written language (*Claywork Compendium*: "If only I understood the beautiful language of SQUEAKSQUEAKSQUUUU!!1!"); Direspeak is the only named language other than leetspeak.
- **Messages**: *Nasty Butler* — "I'm ending this transmission." — L.G.; *Land Shark* — "Plumber... telegram... flowers... candygram..."; *Deadly Striker* letter from the Imperial Army.
- **Photographs** — *Incriminating Photograph*: "Another photo in Dr. Unpeg Noon's distinct style." (1st Ed) / "Another photo by Dr. Unpeg Noon." (Open Beta).
- **Murals** — *Lodge Dock Florist*.

## Medicine and body
- "Luridian medicine" advanced by being able to "see inside people" (*Quotidian Concealer*).
- *Dragon Catheter*, *Dragon Juice*, *Juice Closet* — juice from dragons is used to induce rage; "dragon juice experiments" (*Sleeping Guard*). See `../peoples/seraphim-and-dragons.md`.
- *Ceremonious Groomer* — "Occupational hazards include: thrush, leukoplakia, and fur balls." (Mau groomer)
- *Sickened Sarcophyle* — "While sarcophyles are resilient against most ailments, they are strangely vulnerable to a few, such as anemia."
- Prostheses/animal care: *Prehensile Pirate* ("Dentu-Grip"); *Ferret Oil Coating* ("The word on the street is that this will really help you stand out in a crowd.").
