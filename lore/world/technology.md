# Technology, Majigs and Inventors

Mostly gearsmith-trade material: voidal engineering, "majigs", nodes, golems, war machines.
Leetspeak (e.g. `31f`, `31v35`, `1337`) is the gearsmith "voice" on the cards — see
`../peoples/elves.md`.

---

## Majigs

"Majig" is the card subtype for constructed mechanical beings/devices. Flavor on majigs
frequently names a sub-type by prefixing a word to "-majig".

### Micromajigs (the best-documented technology in the catalog)
Five Holy Heist printings of *Micromajig* carry long encyclopedic text. Full quotes:

- (subtitle "tell me i'm beautiful. lie to me if necessary") "Micromajigs are a humanoid pattern majig, standing perhaps half a foot tall, powered by an identical voidal circuitry core. They are not particularly strong, such as the luteoderm goliath, nor are they astonishingly powerful, such as the runic cannon, but they are unusually prolific. In fact, it is often postulated without question that the micromajig represents the single most common piece of voidal engineering in Luridia today. It is this element of quantity that the architects and inventors find so threatening, offensive, inspiring or simply ludicrous, according to their own views."
- (subtitle "Ambassador M0d3 Un10ck3d") "To understand why these little things would be divisive, one must first understand the fact that the culture of invention in Acrolos is deeply seated in the traditions of voidal engineering practiced by the 31v35, who comprise a very significant portion of the community there. Since the first 31f expatriates settled in Turncrank Gorge in the days of old Marmothoa, they have practiced a small but strict, almost religious, set of tenets governing their art. One of these tenets is the stalwart abhorrence of mass production. The creation of one device that is the exact copy of another is not just uninteresting, but actively offensive to nearly all 31v35.* In stark contrast to this ban, the micromajig is so ubiquitous as to be regarded by many as a mundane nuisance. / * It is not known if the source of this distaste for duplication is grounded in historical experience or a merely religious aversion. Presumably, an answer exists, but it is nearly impossible to have a mature conversation with a male 31f, and nearly all the female 31v35 have sequestered themselves on N1d14, which we call Nidia."
- (subtitle "#MicromajigRevolution") "The origin of the micromajig is shrouded in mystery. No one knows precisely when or how the first micromajig was built. The earliest known written record of a micromajig dates back only six years*, but many anecdotal stories feature their appearance as much as a decade earlier. It is widely believed to be the work of a single rogue gearsmith, referred to in awed or contemptuous voices, as 'The Micromajig Master.' The sheer quantity of micromajigs in the world, however, is far beyond the capacity of a single man to produce and would thus suggest that he has devised an entire apparatus dedicated to the express purpose of producing unfathomable quantities of them. This hypothetical complex of production, hidden somewhere in the Metratropean foothills, is a shrine of the forbidden in Acrolosian culture. / * It is found on the death certificate of one Private Second Class Gozark, kept in the Imperial Army records at Direstock. I have included here the original Direspeak entry for those who might be curious. 'Death-cause : Crushed/smothered (rockslide of small-majigs)'"
- (subtitle "fetch the diamond codpiece!") "What motivates the Master to create these micromajigs is anyone's guess. Is he a folk hero, dedicated to breaking an irrational proscription against mass production? Is he simply a deviant, delighting in his multiplicative transgression against tradition? Perhaps one day the creator of the micromajig and their motives will become known to history. In the meantime, one would be wise to exercise caution when mentioning the subject in the company of tinkers, lest you find yourself subject to impassioned opinion."
- (subtitle "movin' on up") "A few tinkers have speculated, with expressions of wild enthusiasm or disgust, that the micromajigs have learned a way to reproduce themselves. There is no consensus on whether this would occur through crafting them directly as an inventor might, or building machines that build micromajigs, or, as was suggested in at least one moment of perverse fantasy, 'coupling as animals do.' Regardless, these speculations are not given much credit in the wider community."
- Related: *Micromajig Maker* (subtitle "the little ones nag the loudest"); *Micromajig Master* (subtitles "your tiny sacrifices will save us all!" / "we can always make more"); *Micromajig Factory* ("produces micromajigs and/or milkshakes" / "it's a small world, after all"); *Micromajig Recycling Station*; *Micromajig Shipping Container*; *Micromajig Avalanche*; *Micromajigmakermachinemajig* ("I swear they're breeding or something"); *Subdermal Micromajig*; *Improved Micromajig* ("sometimes i'm overpowered" / "It's morphin' time!"); *Wand of Possession* (Seed Saga) — "making micromajigs better one zap at a time".
