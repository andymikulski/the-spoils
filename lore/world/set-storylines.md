# Set Storylines

Four sets carry connected narrative in their flavor text. This file collects the relevant
cards per set. It lists what each card says; it does not stitch the cards into a single
plot beyond what the cards themselves state.

---

## Seed Saga — the fall of Marduun

Cards quoting ***Marduun, a Forgotten History*** (📖) are the backbone; other cards add
character detail.

**Quotations from the book** (full text in `timeline.md`): *A Blazing Zero*, *Adriel, Supreme General of the Imperial Army*, *Death of the Demon Knights*, *Invade!*, *Mau Caravan*, *Mutilated*, *Runic Flee*, *Storm the Gates*.

**Named figures and what each card says**
- *Gideon, the Ultimate Warrior* — subtitle "another white hetero male power fantasy" ⚠; flavor "Child of the [REDACTED]."; subtypes `gideon`, `sword of great justice`.
- *Gilda, Mother of Gideon* — subtitle "it's a wierd family"; flavor "Did you know the father is a [REDACTED]?"
- *Ashara, Gideon's Twin* — subtitle "exactly what it looks like"; flavor "Everyone who isn't us is an enemy."
- *Elzibeth, Military Sorceress* — subtitle "fierce"; flavor "Gideon's wife, commander of the Great Swords, and mother of a new age of horror. For now."
- *Elzibeth, Counter Imperial Countess* — subtitle "in place of an emperor you will set up a queen"; flavor "I shall not be dark, but beautiful and terrible. All shall love me, and despair."
- *Adriel, Defender of Marduun* — subtitle "ultimately, he didn't do a great job"; flavor "He really tried though, bless his heart."
- *Adriel, Supreme General of the Imperial Army* — subtitle "stern, imperious, doomed" (plus the book excerpt).
- *The Emperor Barduse* — subtitle "Gideon is gone. Only Barduse remains."; flavor "Forged in a crucible of desperation and failure."
- *Padamose, Emperor of Marduun* — subtitle "likes: swords, great justice, being emperor"; flavor "dislikes: wizards, lesser mau, being devoured alive by giant tentacle monsters."
- *Athalamund Mangod, The Iron Fist* — subtitle "his jokes are always funny"; flavor "It's because of his punchlines."
- *Gideon's Legacy* — "terror and power runs in their blood"
- *Mud Prison* — "erected to hold in Gideon's sons" / "For their safety. And ours."
- *The Emperor Born* — "inside every Gideon..." / "...there's a demonic dragon-despot waiting to be let out."
- *Woadenworm Gloamspike, Thabbashite Prince* — "I don't make threats...I just tell it like it is." / "Betrayer, assassin, cult leader, loving husband and father of three children."
- *High-Priest of Thabbash* (Seed Saga) — subtitle "it's like worshipping a giant land squid"; flavor "A giant land squid that does nothing but let its tentacles drift silently in the sway of invisible tides."
- *Living God of Thabbash* (Seed Saga) — subtitle "inscrutable, immutable, immortal"
- *Mr. Chesterton* — subtitle "wisdom couched in metaphor"; flavor "Every once in a while we feed him an upholsterer." (card data: `advisor, couch`) ⚠
- *Lecram, Silent Advisor* — subtitle "..."; flavor "..."
- *Imperial Palace* — "it's alright i guess" / "It's where I keep my stuff."
- *Theocratic Senate of Marduun*, *Marduun Baitings and Loan*, *The Brotherhood of the Mau*, *Mau Caravan* (location cards).

Seed Saga also reprints many older cards with new (often jokier) text; those are in `../archive/`.

## Holy Heist — the stolen Living God (part 1)

**Premise as stated by the cards**
- *The Primitive Tribes of Thabbash* (faction) — Living Gods are worshipped by warring tribes in the desert outside Marduun; "Incapable of communication and devoid of intelligence, The Living Gods melt people and animals, cause spontaneous combustion, and transform their followers into monsters. Their follower's only hope for survival will be the use of their own volition..."
- *Final Countdown* (subtitle) — "the mission: steal a stolen god"; flavor "We have six things to do."
- *Early Investment* — "If we're going to make this journey, we'll need money. And lots of it."
- *Gathering of Unlikely Heroes* (subtitle) — "i'll be there for you"; flavor "Well, mostly for me – but also for you... sort of."
- *Close Call* — "You said steal a living god. You said nothing about me getting dead!"
- *Theological Theft* — "Eldridge's therapist suggested he take something for his kleptomania, so he took a god." (subtitle: "that's how you steal a god")
- *Hasty Pursuit* (subtitle) — "thabbashites do not play around when you steal their gods"
- *Profane Proposal* — "... and this my friends, is how we obtain ultimate voidal power."
- *Voidal Humour Redistribution* — "One day, unless we find ourselves a source, Voidal Humour may run out. That's where the god comes in."
- *Expedition Endeavour* (Shade of the Devoured Emperor) — "A group of bad men looking for a very bad thing."

**Gods and Thabbashites in the set**: *Meh, another Living God*, *Hate of Man* (subtitle: "thabbashites worship even the lowest of living gods"), *Personal God Shrine*, *Thabbashite Temple*, *Thabbashite Communicant*, *Thabbashite Deacon*, *Thabbashite Dartist*, *Akashic Coin*, *Akashic Notes*, *Will of a Living God*, *Religious Rejection*, *Offering of False Friendship*, *Confessions of Countless Souls*, *Thabbashite Barbeque* (all in `religion-and-gods.md` / `peoples/thabbashites.md`).

**Named characters in this set**: *Cheats, the Dirty Rotten*, *Chum, the Ugliest Sarume*, *Rudo Mangod, Holder of Light*, *Eldridge Gosche, Godstealer*, *Ignatius Gloamspike* ("not nearly as evil as the other gloamspikes" / "Maybe. Hopefully."), *Loyal Companion* ("jo's right hand mau"), *Zigalburgonglgr, the Stolen God* (subtitle: "fount of unlimited voidal power"). See `../characters/unlikely-heroes.md`.

**Other set flavor**: *Catastrophic Caper* — "There's a thief from Clockman named Pluck, who is often found running amuck…" (limerick; full text in archive).

## Ungodly Mess — the stolen Living God (part 2)

- *Retreat to Mount Mulrich* — "Overwhelmed after the first battle, the Unlikely Heroes escaped to the surrounding mountains, seeking shelter and a way to defeat the Expedition before they could deliver the Living God to Eldridge Gosche. They couldn't shake the feeling of being watched..."
- *Zenobia's Intervention* — subtitle "i cannot let these unlikely heroes be defeated"; flavor: "Your destiny," Zenobia told them, "is to save this world from falling into total darkness like the Swords of Great Justice before." "Great Justice?" Pluck said. "More like Questionable Morality."
- *Zenobia's Succor* (subtitle) — "i've been expecting you. you are our only hope."
- *Zenobian Mechanics Explained* (subtitle) — "i will tell you how to kill a god"; flavor "The rest is up to you."
- *Readied Rifleman* — "The Imperial Army fought Thabbashite, Expedition, and Unlikely Hero alike for control over the Living God, utilizing units of all kinds in the effort."
- *Disposable Meatsack* — "The Thabbashites killed Imperial Army and Counter-Imperial Army nondiscriminately in defense of their god."
- *Wasted Wickedness* (subtitle) — "the god is gone"; flavor: "But the Seraphim remain..."
- *Deicidal Detonation* (subtitle) — "BOOOOOOOM"
- *War Racketeer* (subtitle) — "i'll take forty-over-thirty on the Living God getting blowed up!"
- *Strategic Vision* — "We will steal gosche's gold!"
- *Single Combat* (subtitle) — "rudo vs. gor-blach: FIGHT!"
- *Cunning Ruse* — "abram ka-darbo's greatest trick..."; flavor "Step right up to the Abram Ka-Darbo show, where today I'll be making your living god device disappear."
- *Purloining Performance* (subtitle) — "...stealing a stolen god."
- *Last Minute Weaponization* (subtitle) — "god transport cannon to the face"; *Frantic Jerryrigging* — "god transporter: doubles as a blaster cannon"; *Unaccustomed Inspection* — "staring into the god transport device isn't advised"; *Lead the Assault* — "the horn thundered and the thabbashites came".
- *God's Blessing of …* family (Readiness, Moola, Boost, Foresight, Transfiguration) — "the living god enlivens/enriches/empowers/divinates/endows".
- *Gosche's* location cards (Citadel, Spynarium, Vault, Restoration Fortress, God Amplification Chamber).

- *Yenassa Shipping, LLP* and *Pride of Goru-Goru Gidea* — pirates "Elizibeth's hired pirates" / "a cursed mirror of the Yenassa Shipping, LLP."
- *Fade of the Devoured Emperor*, *Padamose Cultists*, *Apparition Alley* — the Padamose-in-Clockman cards that sit in this set (see also the Shade of the Devoured Emperor section).
- *Geldale, City-Fortress*, *Direstock Stronghold* ("counter-imperial army stronghold"/"nigh-impregnable fortress-city complex"), *Eidolic Cove*, *Wasteland*.
- *Great Mau General* ("imperial army mercenary"), *Abusive Major General* ("considered nice by all imperial standards" / "Barduse feed on friendly flesh nearly as often as that of the enemy.").
- *Customized 31f* ("imperial army abomination" / "The Imperial Army is known to experiment on all manner of man and beast, erasing their memories and making them their pawns.").
- *Elite Acrolos Engineer* ("screw the rules" / "I have money."), *Carnival Whipmaster*, *Acrolosian Outpost #30016968A*, *Fleet Majig*.
