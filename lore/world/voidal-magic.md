# Voidal Magic, Ritual and Arcana

Everything the cards say about how magic works, who practices it, and what it costs.
The cards are comedic and rarely explain mechanisms; this file keeps the descriptions as given.

---

## The Void and Voidal Humour

- **"The Void"** — *Twisted Marionette*: "Little Suzy's teachers told her not to stare too deep into the Void. She should've listened."
- **Voidal Humour** — *Voidal Bask*: "Living Gods are founts of Voidal Humour-- the raw stuff of magick. Voidal Humour is extremely volatile, and can result in mutation, mutilation, and mutitilation." (subtitle: "poisoned by proximity to the fount")
- *Magic Vault* (subtitle "gradual enhancer of voidal currency") — "You see, feedback is the key to our wealth. The vaults are a tremendous expenditure, true, but the interest they generate simply by storing the voidal humour in its materialized state will eventually pay for the investment many times over. Never have the rich gotten richer with less effort."
- *Voidal Humour Redistribution* (subtitle "top men toiling away") — "One day, unless we find ourselves a source, Voidal Humour may run out. That's where the god comes in."
- *Unfortunate Fortune Hunter* — "Arcanist Lords amassed untold wealth as a result of the commodification of voidal humour."
- *Runic Flee* — Gloamspike's "sons would one day discover Voidal Humour ushering in the modern age of commerce and industry."
- *Zigalburgonglgr, the Stolen God* (subtitle) — "fount of unlimited voidal power"
- *Index of Natural Forces* — "Mau invest not only in property and jewels; they maintain interests in the fabric of reality itself."
- *Dandy Day-Trader* (subtitle) — "trafficker of metaphysical currency"; flavor: "Mortimer made a fortune investing in the gravity IPO. Unfortunately, he lost it all on a rainy day due to a massive overextension in the sunlight market." ⚠
- Voidal pollution: *Lugubrian Poacher* — "decades of voidal pollution have made [the wastelands of Lugubria] host to many dangerous monsters."
- Voidal waste: *Corrosive Waste Dispenser* (subtitle: "voidal waste remover").
- Voidal storms: *Arcanist's Almanac* — "Smarch 5th – Strong voidal storms from the southeast. Wear a bowler hat."

## Arcana and the Arcanists

- **Arcanist discipline/personality**: *Deceptive Muddler* — "An Arcanist keeps his things 'just so' with good reason. Nothing annoys him more than repositioning." *Obsessive Compulsion* (subtitle) — "a place for everything and everything in its place". *Master Ritualist* (subtitle) — "scheduler of obsession".
- *Compulsive Creeper* — "It appears that after the victim was murdered, the suspect cleaned the room, did the dishes, and organized his books alphabetically."
- **Arcanology**: *Arcana Experiment* (2nd Ed) — "Stand back, I'm going to try science!"; *Arcanology Recruitment Officer* (subtitle) — "a warning and a promise" / "Ladies love a chitinous monstrosity."; *Elementary Elemental* — "Little Suzy suspected that today would have been a good day to stay home from the Arcanology Academy."
- **Arcanist Lords**: *Seance Ritualist* (subtitle) — "arcanist lords harbor untold power"; *Unfortunate Fortune Hunter* ("Arcanist Lords amassed untold wealth…"); *Eldridge Gosche* (subtitle) — "lurid Lugubrian leader".
- **Arcana field**: *Ominous Oozling* — "never meant to come into contact with those outside the Arcana field."
- **Arcanology Branch**: Clockman's "isolated towers" (see `geography.md`).
- Bounties: *Lugubrian Poacher* — "the arcanists have begun to find uses for these beasts and will pay bounties to anyone foolish enough to pursue them."
- *Arcanology/Arcana Department* (subtitle) — "where suspicion breeds confidence"; flavor "Take two lefts, an up, and a sideways...it's the third door on your right."

### Quotidian magic
"Quotidian" is a recurring modifier for a family of ritual-based abilities.
- *Quotidian Concealer* — "Being able to actually see inside people was a massive step forward in Luridian medicine - and Imperical torture."
- *Quotidian Quagmire*, *Quotidian Transposition*, *Quotidian Misfire* ("If only I'd remembered to turn the knob 17 times instead of 16 and a half!"), *Quotidian Assassination* (subtitle "a convenient and cowardly solution"; flavor is a wordy repeating chant about shattering glass and drawn cords), *Quotidian Disappearance* ("Political enemies of Amalforact often attract the unwanted attentions of the firm's public compliance officers, who do their job with perverted glee.").
- *Ritualized Destruction* — subtitle "quotidian is for the mundane"; flavor "True ritualists are anything but."
- *Voidal Webbing* — "residue of quotidian ritual".
- *Mephitic Nullifier*, *Vexing Nullifier*, *Quotidian Ejector* (subtitle "trivially rejecting whatever he chooses"), *Quotidian Virtuoso* ("Queen to L5 and the tower falls. Your move.").
- *The Lugubrian Council of Quotidian Affairs* (subtitle) — "we need a god to study and exploit... all in favor say 'aye'!"
- *12 Man Brunch of Doom* — "If even one thing is wrong, even the slightest thing, a bad phrase, spilt salt, weak posture, anything at all...the consequences can be horrific. Exploded, melted, turned inside out, consumed by fire, filled with pudding...the 12 Man Brunch has finished many a cocksure arcanist." — Werner Geisen, Master Ritualist.
- Named rituals (all tactics): Ritual of the Knob & Pull ("Twist to the left dammit! Don't you remember what happened to Jenkins!?"); Ritual of the Ominous Urn ("The nameless one twisted his inhuman appendage unspeakably, as an ineffable sound filled the unfathomable, indescribable sky..."); Ritual of the Screaming Cheese ("Is the cheese screaming, Clarice?"); Ritual of the Shattering Teeth ("I want you to know, this hurts you more than it hurts me."); Ritual of the Sucking Skull; Ritual of the Double Monkey; Arcane Monkey Ritual; Ecto-Distillation ("complex magical process"); Eccentric Inquisition.

### Libraries, archives, reliquaries
