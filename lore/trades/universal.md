# Universal / Voidal Cards

Threshold icon **V** ("voidal") appears on 29 of them and on many Thabbash-themed cards of
other trades.

## The five resources
Staple resource names: **Obsession, Greed, Elitism, Deception, Rage** (1st Edition); upgraded names — Neurosis, Exploitation, Arrogance, Treachery, Violence (Seed) and Obsessive Concentration, Greedy Profiteering, Elitist Upgrade, Deceptive Domain, Raging Ferocity (Holy Heist). The initials line up with the five trades' threshold letters (O, G, E, D, R). No flavor text covers them.
