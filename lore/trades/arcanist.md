## What the cards say about Arcanists as people
- Obsessive: "An Arcanist keeps his things 'just so' with good reason. Nothing annoys him more than repositioning." (*Deceptive Muddler*); *Obsessive Compulsion* "a place for everything and everything in its place"; *Master Ritualist* "scheduler of obsession".
- Ritual-bound and precarious: *12 Man Brunch of Doom* (Werner Geisen, Master Ritualist) — wrong salt, posture or phrase can leave "many a cocksure arcanist" exploded, melted or "filled with pudding".
- Wealthy and powerful as a class ("Arcanist Lords"): *Unfortunate Fortune Hunter*, *Seance Ritualist*.
- Bureaucratic in tone: *Arcana Department*, *Lugubrian Council of Quotidian Affairs*, *Officially Offensive Overseer* ("more offensive than official" / "but less officious than offensive.").
- Cruel professions: *Lingamite Torture Monk*, *Unbearable Torture* ("Do you hear that? That is the sound of ultimate suffering."), *Quotidian Concealer* (Imperical torture).
- Mau distrust: *Unfortunate Fortune Hunter* — "Untrusting of the banker Mau class and unable to protect his treasure in person, Gosche hid witch clocks…". *Underwhelming Performance* (an Arcanist tactic) — "Sometimes the Mau have the right idea. (but don't tell them we said that!)"

## Tone
Gothic horror played as workplace comedy; wizards are a running joke ("ugh, wizards"); monsters are mundane domestic hazards (armchairs, mansions, lamps).
