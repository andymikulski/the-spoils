## What the cards say
- Army culture is brutal and darkly comic (`../world/society-law-and-money.md`): cannibal rations, trophy collecting, dragon-juice experiments, "blowed up" casualty letters.
- The Marduun-era heroes and villains are Warlord cards: Gideon, Adriel, Athalamund, Giada, Octo-Ba-Ba, Elzibeth, Padamose, Barduse (`../characters/marduun-era.md`).
- Direstock is the Warlord stronghold; Geldale the counter-imperial one (`../world/geography.md`).
- Dragons and their riders (`../peoples/seraphim-and-dragons.md`).
- Thabbashite fighters (Gor-blach, Host, Warmonger, Warbeast) (`../peoples/thabbashites.md`).
