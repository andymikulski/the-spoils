## What the cards say
- Home base: Acrolos (six-island chain), plus campuses on mainland Metatropea; elves are a major part of the community (`../peoples/elves.md`, `../world/geography.md`).
- Culture of invention: one-of-a-kind craftsmanship is almost religious; mass production is offensive — hence the micromajig controversy (`../world/technology.md`).
- Wealth and arms trade: *Jo Ciendeilio, M07h3r 0f 1337* "provides technology in bulk so the rich can resell each piece to the highest warmongering bidder"; *Elite Acrolos Engineer* ("screw the rules" / "I have money."); *Acrolosian Outpost #30016968A*.
- Tournament and gaming culture: *Elite Team Registration* ("legendary in their frivolity and ferocity"), *Hall of Conventions*, *Operation 1337 H4x0rz*.
- Social stereotype: "it is nearly impossible to have a mature conversation with a male 31f" (*Micromajig*).
- Magic here is runic and clay-based (`../world/voidal-magic.md`).
