## What the cards say
- Clockman is the Rogue home: *Clockman Pickpocket*, *Torchlight District*, *Hangdog Alley*, *Torchlight Safehouse*, *Apparition Alley*, *Murderous Kleptomaniac* ("Some Clockman criminals are licensed professionals. Others are less... legitimate."), *Clockman City Shuffle* (Pluck's song).
- Crime as a trade: heists, forgery, gambling, fencing (`../world/society-law-and-money.md`).
- Carnival and performance folk are Rogues (`../world/daily-life-and-culture.md`).
- Sarcophyles (`../peoples/sarcophyles.md`) and Thabbashite assassins/urchins sit here.
- Unlikely Heroes' core characters are Rogues: Pluck, Cheats, Abram Ka-darbo Alakazin, Chum, Cicibeo, Lecram, Bottlebeard Brandyshanks (`../characters/unlikely-heroes.md`).
- "Miners": *Fired Hand*, *Muscle Tribe of Danger and Excellence*, *Resistance Fighter* ("We're freedom fighters from the future.") — see `../factions/factions-and-organizations.md` (*Union of Unextraordinary Miners*).
