# Factions and Organizations

Two kinds of entry: **faction cards** (the game's starting cards, 23 distinct names) and
**in-world organizations** named in flavor, subtitles or card names. Faction cards carry
almost no flavor; most of what is known comes from their names and from other cards.

---

## Faction cards

### Holy Heist
- **Unlikely Heroes** — no flavor. Cards that name "the Unlikely Heroes": *Gathering of Unlikely Heroes*, *Retreat to Mount Mulrich*, *Zenobia's Intervention*, *Readied Rifleman*, *Feckless Chronicler* (subtitle "singing the song of the unlikely heroes"), *Zenobia's Succor*.
- **Lugubrian Council Expedition Division** — no flavor. See "Lugubrian Council / the Expedition" below.
- **The Primitive Tribes of Thabbash** — flavor: "In the desert outside of Marduun, the warring Thabbash tribes worship their Living Gods. Incapable of communication and devoid of intelligence, The Living Gods melt people and animals, cause spontaneous combustion, and transform their followers into monsters. Their follower's only hope for survival will be the use of their own volition..." See `../peoples/thabbashites.md`.

### Seed (one faction per trade)
Card data pairs each faction with a starting resource; names are the only text.
| Faction | Starting resource | Matching trade (by resource) |
|---|---|---|
| Moist Cave of the Darkpump | Obsession | Arcanist |
| Order of the Golden String | Greed | Banker |
| Alliance of Handy Weirdos | Elitism | Gearsmith |
| Drowned Catacombs | Deception | Rogue |
| Hall of Great Justice | Rage | Warlord |
(Also reprinted in Seed Saga.) Note: the trade ↔ resource pairing comes from the five threshold letters o/g/e/d/r on the cards of each trade; no flavor describes it. The "Hall of Great Justice" name echoes *Swords of Great Justice* (a subtype); the cards do not link them.

### Seed 3
- **The Evil Alliance** and **Slightly Less Evil Alliance** (subtitle "(of Justice)") — card text refers to "Heroes", "the Villain" and a "Temptation Pile" (mechanics only).
- **Imperial Army** and **Counter-Imperial Army** (subtitle "(other side starts face-up)") — the two-sided faction.
- **The Society of Magical Propriety**, **The Knitting Coalition** (subtitle "(doesn't exist)"), **The Open R&D Faction**, **The Union of Unextraordinary Miners** — marked "cannot be used in tournament play". Treat as joke/meta entries.

### Seed Saga
- **Defenders of Marduun** and **Legions of Chaos** — no flavor. Marduun is the fallen capital (`../world/timeline.md`).

### Universal
- **The Tournament Faction** — the generic faction; no lore.

## In-world organizations

### Armies and martial orders
- **Imperial Army** — see `../world/society-law-and-money.md` (Supreme General Adriel; Grand General Praxis; Direstock records; experiments on "man and beast"; mercenary conscription; "Headball" championship with the Counter-Imperial Army). Also *Imperial Thaumaturge* (Arcanist `wizard`), *Imperial Palace* (location: "It's where I keep my stuff.").
- **Counter-Imperial Army** — *Geldale, City-Fortress* (subtitle "counter-imperial army stronghold"); *Elzibeth, Counter Imperial Countess*; *Disposable Meatsack*.
- **Great Swords** — *Elzibeth, Military Sorceress*: "commander of the Great Swords"; *Adriel, Supreme General of the Imperial Army* calls Adriel "the former Great Sword Adriel".
- **Swords of Great Justice** — Warlord/Banker/Rogue subtype `sword of great justice` (Adriel, Gideon, Athalamund Mangod, Giada, Iohane, Octo-Ba-Ba, Vilghame). *Zenobia's Intervention*: "total darkness like the Swords of Great Justice before." *Padamose, Emperor of Marduun*: Warlord text buffs "sword of great justice" characters (card text).
- **Blades of Questionable Morality** — subtype `blade of questionable morality` (Ignatius, Rudo, Eustace, Jo, Abram Ka-darbo Alakazin). *Wasteland*: "The Blades escaped, just barely…"; *Zenobia's Intervention*: "More like Questionable Morality."
- **Demon Knights** — subtype `demon knight` (Geiseric, Ipsighast, Merbegon). Tactics: *Enter The Demon Knights*, *Death of the Demon Knights*.
- **Lingamites** — `../peoples/humans-and-others.md`.
- **Pandiatus Army** — *Crimson Pandiatus*.
- **Berserker Corps**, **Ruthless Platoon**, **Suicide Squad**, **Special Forces**, **Cannon Squadron** — unit names on cards.
- **Guardforce** (gearsmith subtype `guardforce`), **T Force 5** ("Teenage Towering Titan Trampling Team", *T Force 5 Megascoutmajig*), **Kaisers** (`kaiser`).
- **Clockman Guard** — "infamously corrupt" (*Pervasive Bodyguard*).
- **Acrolos security** — *53cur17y 5y573m*: "protecting Acrolos so you don't have to".

### The Lugubrian Council / the Expedition
- *The Lugubrian Council of Quotidian Affairs* (Arcanist `advisor`, unique; Shade of the Devoured Emperor) — subtitle: "we need a god to study and exploit... all in favor say 'aye'!"
- *Lugubrian Council Expedition Division* (Holy Heist faction card).
- "The Expedition" in flavor: *Expedition Advisor* ("I thought you said he was an intern."), *Expedition Endeavour* ("A group of bad men looking for a very bad thing."), *Expedition AutoBlind*, *Retreat to Mount Mulrich* ("defeat the Expedition before they could deliver the Living God to Eldridge Gosche"), *Readied Rifleman*, *Thabbashite Barbeque* ("The expedition grew silent…"), *Ursine Assassin* ("An amazing asset on an expedition"), *Spurious Anthropologist* (`advisor`; "just work with me here, look at all of this"), *Early Investment* ("If we're going to make this journey, we'll need money. And lots of it.").
