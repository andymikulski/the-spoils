# Characters of the Living God Sets (Holy Heist / Ungodly Mess) and the Pluck Subtype

The *Unlikely Heroes* are a faction card, but no card lists its members. Below: (1) who the
flavor *explicitly* ties to the Living God episode, (2) the `pluck` subtype group, (3) other
named characters from those sets. Membership in the "Unlikely Heroes" is **not** asserted
for anyone unless the card says so.

---

## Explicit ties
- **Pluck** — *Zenobia's Intervention*: "'Your destiny,' Zenobia told them, 'is to save this world from falling into total darkness like the Swords of Great Justice before.' 'Great Justice?' Pluck said. 'More like Questionable Morality.'" Also *Catastrophic Caper* (limerick naming "a thief from Clockman named Pluck"), *Clockman City Shuffle* (verse signed "— Pluck"), and the *Pluck* card (subtitle "crafty exploiter of scenes").
- **Zenobia** — *Zenobia's Intervention* (above); *Zenobia's Succor* ("i've been expecting you. you are our only hope."); *Zenobian Mechanics Explained* ("i will tell you how to kill a god"); card *Zenobia, Artificer* (Gearsmith `sorceress`, "legendary witch, keeper of secrets"); *Zenobia's Tower* ("on the spectral face of mt. mulrich"). See `lugubria-and-the-void.md`.
- **Eldridge Gosche** — *Theological Theft*: "Eldridge's therapist suggested he take something for his kleptomania, so he took a god." *Retreat to Mount Mulrich*: the Heroes want to defeat the Expedition "before they could deliver the Living God to Eldridge Gosche."
- **Abram Ka-darbo Alakazin** — *Cunning Ruse*: "Step right up to the Abram Ka-Darbo show, where today I'll be making your living god device disappear."
- **Rudo Mangod** and **Gor-blach the Endowed** — *Single Combat* (subtitle "rudo vs. gor-blach: FIGHT!"); *Rudo Mangod, Holder of Light* — "Only a true Mangod heir could free the sword from its resting place. In one swift movement, Rudo claimed his birthright to badassery and generations of stored power. This is going to end very poorly for somebody."
- **Jo Ciendeilio** and **Loyal Companion** — see `acrolos-and-gearsmiths.md`.
- **Eustace** — *Reluctant Reconciliation* (Jo offers "to Eustace by restoring his broken majig").
- **Feckless Chronicler** (Rogue `performer`; subtitle "singing the song of the unlikely heroes").
- **Zigalburgonglgr, the Stolen God** — the Living God itself.
