## Eldridge Gosche
- *Eldridge Gosche* (2nd Ed; Arcanist `ritualist`) — subtitle "lurid Lugubrian leader".
- *Eldridge Gosche, Godstealer* (Holy Heist; `ritualist, eldridge`) — subtitle "a great mind bent beneath the weight of its own greatness".
- *Theological Theft* — "Eldridge's therapist suggested he take something for his kleptomania, so he took a god."
- *Retreat to Mount Mulrich* — the Expedition was to "deliver the Living God to Eldridge Gosche."
- *Unfortunate Fortune Hunter* — "Gosche hid witch clocks throughout Goru-Goru Gidea to protect his riches."
- *Gosche's* locations (Ungodly Mess): God Amplification Chamber ("where the god goes, so goes eldridge"), Retained Earnings Vault, Solar Citadel ("life from the void"), Spynarium ("hidden by magick and ritual"), Restoration Fortress ("regenerative voidal experimentation"); tactic *Gosche's Voidal Frequency Emission* ("like shrievers to an emission device" / "Gosche always says that."); *Strategic Vision* ("we will steal gosche's gold!"); *Voidal Tactician* ("gosche's trusted advisor"); *Gloamspike-Gosche Bridge*.
