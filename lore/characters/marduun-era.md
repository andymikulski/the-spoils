# The Marduun Era: Swords, Demon Knights and the Fall of the Empire

Named, unique characters tied by their cards to the war for Marduun and the Marmothoan
Empire. Entries list what each card says. Relationships are limited to those a card
states outright. Context: `../world/timeline.md`.

Card-subtype groups: **`sword of great justice`** (7 cards: Adriel Defender of Marduun,
Gideon, Athalamund Mangod, Vilghame Manewaft, Iohane the Convex, Giada the Elegant,
Octo-Ba-Ba the Devourer) · **`demon knight`** (Geiseric the Souleater, Ipsighast the Lustful,
Merbegon the Insatiable Taxidermist).
