# Contradictions, Variants and Ambiguities

Left unresolved on purpose. When building content, pick one version knowingly.

---

## Same figure, different framing
1. **Padamose — Marduun or Marmothoa; ruler or Mau**
   - *Padamose, Emperor of Marduun* (card subtypes `padamose, emperor, mau`).
   - *Mau Caravan* 📖: the Marmothoan Empire was indebted to "mixed-blood relatives of the Emperor - the Mau"; Padamose absolved the debts and forced them to flee to Clockman.
   - *Executive Order #6370u7*: Padamose "expelled the Mau from Marduun".
   - *Apparition Alley*: Padamose "wondering how his beloved Marmothoa had given rise to such a vile and dangerous place."
   - *Padamose* dislikes "lesser mau".
   - The cards use both "Marduun" and Marmothoa / the Marmothoan Empire; none says they are the same place or that one replaced the other.
2. **Adriel** — "Defender of Marduun" vs "former Great Sword Adriel, having forsaken king and realm" vs "Supreme General of the Imperial Army"; the cards never state a sequence. The 📖 line also makes him responsible for "the demise of his greatest ally Gideon".
3. **Barduse** — a person ("The Emperor Barduse"; "the newly born Barduse") and a creature type ("barduse" in *Bladefist Doomwing*, *Flaming Barduse*, *Really Big Barduse*, *Abusive Major General*), and *Lingamite Descendant* says Gideon's children "were turned into barduse".
4. **Gideon** — "Child of the [REDACTED]." (*Gideon*) and "Did you know the father is a [REDACTED]?" (*Gilda*); *Ashara* is "Gideon's Twin" ; *Elzibeth* is "Gideon's wife". All presented as stated, none expanded.
5. **Elzibeth** — "Gideon's wife, commander of the Great Swords"; "Counter Imperial Countess"; delivered Marduun to the Thabbashites with Gloamspike (*Marduun Ruins*); "Elizibeth's hired pirates" (*Yenassa Shipping, LLP*); "the Countess" (*Strategic Vision*); has a peak named for her (*Geldale, City-Fortress*).
6. **Gloamspike** — fled to Lugubria after the final confrontation (*Runic Flee*) yet also helped deliver Marduun to the Thabbashites 150 years before the present (*Marduun Ruins*); several other Gloamspikes exist (*Woadenworm*, *Ignatius*: "not nearly as evil as the other gloamspikes").
7. **The Emperor's ghost** — *Fade of the Devoured Emperor*: "his mission fulfilled" and "drifted to his final rest"; *Shade of the Devoured Emperor*: "back on unfinished business"; *Abandoned Testing Complex*: "the Emperor's ghost had meant for them to find".

## Places
8. **Marduun after the fall** — *Marduun Ruins* (a ruined mecca for tentacled god-things) coexists with location cards for a functioning *Theocratic Senate of Marduun* and *Marduun Baitings and Loan*; no date is given.
9. **Where is the Living God episode?** The Thabbash tribes live "in the desert outside of Marduun"; the Expedition goes to Mount Mulrich and Lugubria; the "Elizibeth" pirates operate out of Yenassa/Goru-Goru Gidea. No map ties these together.
10. **Nidia / Nidian Sea** — *Nidia* ("N1d14, which we call Nidia") is a homeland of female elves; *Nidian Sea* borders Acrolos. The cards do not say whether the names are linked.
11. **Direstock** — a "castle" (*Anxious Oaf*), "fortress-city complex" (*Direstock Stronghold*), and the site of Imperial Army records.

## Dates and scale
12. **How old is the micromajig?** "The earliest known written record of a micromajig dates back only six years" (*Micromajig*) yet the same text puts them in "Luridia today" as "the single most common piece of voidal engineering".
13. **Time scales** — 150 years since Marduun's fall (*Marduun Ruins*); "decades of voidal pollution" in Lugubria (*Lugubrian Poacher*); Padamose's ghost in Clockman (*Apparition Alley*, *Fade of the Devoured Emperor*). No card relates these spans to each other.
14. **Elf ages** — "6732 years young" (*Beefy Researcher*), "300 years in runecrafting school" (*Research Assistant*), "eighty years" for a dissertation (*Research Intern*).
15. **Clockman's size** — a "small mining town on the coast" in *Mau Caravan*, home of "Clockman's most powerful investment bank" in *Goldmau Sacks Representative*, with sky docks (*Clockman Skydocks*).

## Reprint wording changes
Many reprints change the flavor (e.g. *Ominous Oozling*: 1st Ed uses the Ms. Dowdy caption, Open Beta uses the "Bred in the depths of darkest Lugubria" line; *Swarm of Gnats* and *Eldridge Gosche* also differ between printings). The archive keeps every distinct version with its set list.
