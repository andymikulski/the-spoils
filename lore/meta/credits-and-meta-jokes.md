## Design-team and fourth-wall jokes
- *Contrabulous Fabtraption* (Seed Saga) — "some day we will do a full art version of this card" / "Art? Where we're going, we don't need art."
- *Titillated Mooncalf* — "secret languages are so Seed II".
- *Drivey Car*, *Time Portal*, *Surprising Nuisance* (Seed) — flavor is a list of serial numbers like "7VV0 – 016/200, 009/165…" (card-print numbers written in leetspeak).
- *Cock Block* (Seed Saga) — "just what the name says" / "Nothing Else."
- *Wand of Possession* — "No, they don't gain the 'evil' subtype." (rules reference)
- *Luteoderm Goliath* (2nd Ed) — "Immediately destroyed if your opponent plays Luteoderm David." (rules reference)
- *Gilda, Mother of Gideon* — "Did you know the father is a [REDACTED]?" and *Gideon, the Ultimate Warrior* subtitle "another white hetero male power fantasy" — jokey lines that are also the only text on those cards; they are recorded in `../characters/marduun-era.md` with ⚠.

- "My preciousssss" (*Fwing Snipe*)
- "Stand back, I'm going to try science!" (*Arcana Experiment*)
- "It's a Trap!" (*It's a Trap!*), "Great Scott!" (*Shocking Reappearance*), "Contrariwise, if it was so, it might be…" (*Dweedle Tee*), "To pee or not to pee" (*Scintillating Chamber Pot*), "Imagine that. Dragons. Much wow."
