*list of what exists on cards*; combining items is up to you. Cross-check facts against
the people/trade files before using.

### Patterns
- **Mau** — absurdly formal or comic surnames; finance/legal puns (Gristwalter, Tromwibbler, Blatherscythe).
- **Elves** — leetspeak names: F14xu5, Cr4ck13, 5n4p, P0p, 700160x, 633fy 31f.
- **Thabbashites** — hyphenated or apostrophe names: Hassan-i, Gor-blach, Woadenworm, Zigalburgonglgr (god).
- **Warlords** — compound-violence names: Bladefist Doomwing, Skullcrush Steamroller, Dethmürderblüdgeon, Hungermaw Deephate.
- **Titles/epithets**: "X, the Y" or "Y of Z" — Eldridge Gosche, Godstealer · Framptle Tromwibbler, Lord of Bling · Hextilda, the Exiled Crone · Giada, the Elegant · Cheats, the Dirty Rotten · Octo-Ba-Ba, the Devourer.

## 3. Voice and speech registers (observed)
- **Mau** — legal/financial jargon, euphemism, sales pitch ("my $PLATINUM DEAL$…", "The terms of our severance package ensure a high rate of employee retention."), cat idiom ("kitty", "paws").
- **Arcanists** — fussy, ritual-obsessed, ominous (Werner Geisen's list of ways a brunch can go wrong; "Little Suzy's teachers told her not to stare too deep into the Void.").
- **Gearsmiths / elves** — leetspeak (`j00`, `n00b`, `ph33r`, `pwnt`), inventor boasts, sincere technical prose in long-form cards.
- **Rogues** — wise-cracking, con-artist patter, limericks and songs (*Clockman City Shuffle*, *Catastrophic Caper*).
- **Warlords** — blustering insults, gallows humor, military bureaucracy ("regrets to inform you…blowed up").
- **Thabbashites** — described by others as mysterious; speech is rarely quoted (*High-Priest of Thabbash*: "Something something something daaark siiide.").
