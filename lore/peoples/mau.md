# The Mau

The most heavily documented people in the catalog: 140 cards carry the subtype `mau`
(almost all Banker). Cards use "Mau", "housecats", "kitty"/"Big kitty", "Tradesmau",
"Goldmau", "Lesser Mau" and cat imagery (catnip, paws, fur balls). Whether Mau are
anthropomorphic cats is never stated outright, but nothing contradicts it and the cards use
cat-related language throughout.

---

## What the cards say they are

- *Mau Caravan* (Seed Saga; quoting *Marduun, a Forgotten History*) — "mixed-blood relatives of the Emperor" of the Marmothoan Empire; Padamose "declared all debts to 'housecats' absolved, thereby forcing their immediate flee across the desert."
- *Padamose, Emperor of Marduun* — card subtypes `padamose, emperor, mau`. Flavor: "likes: swords, great justice, being emperor / dislikes: wizards, lesser mau, being devoured alive by giant tentacle monsters."
- **"Lesser Mau"** — *Eidolic Cove*: "warlike tribes of barbarous Lesser Mau" in Goru-Goru Gidea; also in Padamose's dislikes (above). A "Mau class" distinction is implied elsewhere: *Unfortunate Fortune Hunter* — "Untrusting of the banker Mau class…"
- **Housecats** — *Mau Caravan* (above). *Timid Mau* — "Kitty gonna cry? Big kitty gonna cry?" (subtitle "don't be such a pussy").
- **Padamose is a Mau and an Emperor** (card subtypes) *and* the one who expelled the Mau (*Mau Caravan*; *Executive Order #6370u7*) — see `../meta/contradictions.md`.

## Origins and arrival in Clockman
- *Mau Caravan* (above): destination "A small mining town on the coast called Clockman…"
- *Revivatate* (2nd Ed) — "In Clockman, they used to have a saying, 'Only two things are certain in life: death and taxes.' Then the Mau arrived."
- *Executive Order #6370u7* (Seed Saga) — "With Executive Order #6370u7, Padamose expelled the Mau from Marduun, clearing the way for a more sinister cult to form in their stead."

## Culture and values
- **Wealth is central**: *Cathédrale de Mau* (subtitle) — "money is god, greed is king, and corruption runs the rest". *Rate Hike* — "All money belongs to a Mau somewhere, whether the money realizes it or not." *Index of Natural Forces* — "Mau invest not only in property and jewels; they maintain interests in the fabric of reality itself." *Extortify* (2nd Ed) — "Involuntary donations are a well-established part of Mau social doctrine." *Pyramid Scheme* — "A mau always comes out on top." *Enlightened Mau* — "Money is a terrible master, but an excellent servant." (subtitle "agnostic ascetic")
- **Private security**: *Pervasive Bodyguard* (2nd Ed) — "Since the Clockman Guard is infamously corrupt. Mau often feel the need to contract private security from their own kind to safeguard their personal interests."
- **Desires**: *Clandestine Resort* — "Every Mau secretly desires their own private island, where the feasts are endless and the beaches are littered with catnip." (subtitle "ah...at last! more pudding!")
- **Catnip**: *Armored Keep* (subtitle) — "protected store of gold, jewels, and catnip"; *Club Mau Bouncer* — "there's no petting in the Catnip Room" / "None."; *Grandiose Resurrector* (Seed Saga) — "who dares disturb my slumb- ooh, is that catnip?"
- **Appearance/grooming**: *Ceremonious Groomer* — "Occupational hazards include: thrush, leukoplakia, and fur balls." (Seed Saga subtitle "licking perfection"); *Kitten Mittons* — "Finally, an elegant comfortable mitton...for mau!"; *Dwarvish Grimalkin* — "judge me by my size, do you?"
- **Sociability**: *Mau Party* — "it's not a party until somebody gets devoured" / "After we run them over, we stop the car and eat them." ⚠; *Balthassar Gristwalter* — "parties so swanky the emperor is ashamed to attend".
- **Piety**: priest-type Mau — see `../world/religion-and-gods.md`.
- **Non-Mau opinions of Mau**: *Underwhelming Performance* (an Arcanist tactic) — "Sometimes the Mau have the right idea. (but don't tell them we said that!)"; *Thabbashite Financier* — "The value of gold transcends culture."; *Special Forces* — "I'd crawl into a mau's toilet if it kept me out of sight."
- **Mau institutions**: *The Brotherhood of the Mau* ("they run the show" / "Extorting empires since before it was cool."), *Cathédrale de Mau*, *Mau Caravan*, *Goldmau Sacks* (see `../factions/factions-and-organizations.md`).

## Occupations on Mau cards

assassin, bureaucrat, merchant, hunter, tough guy, dancer, groomer, cleric, appraiser,
auditor, bodyguard, general. (Card subtypes alongside `mau`.)
