# Monsters, Beasts and Creatures

Lurkers (46 cards, all Arcanist), monstrosities (31), beasts (31), familiars, insects,
warbeasts. This file collects what the cards say about each. Dragon-types are in
`seraphim-and-dragons.md`; constructs in `../world/technology.md`.

---

## Origin / habitat statements
- Lugubria — *Lugubrian Poacher*: "The wastelands of Lugubria were never hospitable, but decades of voidal pollution have made them host to many dangerous monsters. In a strange twist, the arcanists have begun to find uses for these beasts and will pay bounties to anyone foolish enough to pursue them."
- *Ominous Oozling* (Open Beta) — "Bred in the depths of darkest Lugubria, these creatures were never meant to come into contact with those outside the Arcana field."
- *Gargantuan Garbler* (2nd Ed) — "'Ambush by voidal abomination' is the leading cause of child mortality in Lugubria. Authorities remain unconcerned."
- Thabbash — *Monstrous Lassitude*: Thabbashite mages "'enlist' the services of horrific creatures of titanic proportions".
- Desert — *Gruesome Sand Beaver*: "Sand beavers are known for random acts of violence upon the unsuspecting traveler. They live in the desert to avoid the whole dam business." ; *Craniata Nester* ("malevolent desert terror"); *Arrakis Eternity* ("horror of the sands"); *Thabbashite Desert Creature* ("powered by the souls of its victims").
- Voidal pollution as source: *Voidal Bask* — Living Gods are "founts of Voidal Humour"; *Interpret the Void* — side effects include "tail growth".
- Luridia — *Thabbashite Organist*: "This odd gargantuan relic is actually alive. Nature in Luridia is not without its bizarre tendencies evidenced in this case by the instrumental biology of this unmoving creature."

## Lurkers (Arcanist; subtype `lurker`)
- *Lugubrian Octopod* — "it's watching you"
- *Ominous Oozling* — "squishing is the last thing you'll hear"; 1st Ed flavor is a caption about Ms. Clotidia Dowdy (see `geography.md`).
- *Puddle of Goo* — "out of the drainpipe and into our tummies!"; "According to Meldring's 'Edible Slimes of Lugubria,' the role of the human digestive system in the reproductive cycle of the hallucinogenic sewerslime is as vital as it is ambiguous."
- *Land Shark* (2nd Ed) — "who is it?" / "Plumber... telegram... flowers... candygram... I'm only a dolphin ma'am." ⚠; *Man Shark* — "distant relative of the land shark".
- *Sneaky Mansion* — "did you hear that?" / "Not a creature was stirring, not even a house."
- *Predatory Lamp* — "Bringing light to where the sun don't shine in all kinds of nasty ways. / I should have been sad when the lamp went out, but instead I was delighted."
- *Infatuated Armchair* — "The armchair's thirst for heiny was boundless and unfathomable. We eventually had to put it down."
- *Kinky Asp* — "Only slightly less deadly than the dreaded bent mamba."
- *Venomous Sacspider* — "As pets they're surprisingly affectionate, but fragile. Slip-ups are always bad news; just a drop of their juice is enough to liquefy a cow."
- *Violating Anomaly* (`muddler, lurker`) — "I thought I was a goner, but turns out they only wanted my tobacco."
- *Cloaked Creeper* — "'try to get to the buffet table'" / "Ah, Lord Frothingslosh...don't you look...dashing..."
- *Compulsive Creeper* — "It appears that after the victim was murdered, the suspect cleaned the room, did the dishes, and organized his books alphabetically."
- *Elementary Elemental* — "adorably horrible"; "Little Suzy suspected that today would have been a good day to stay home from the Arcanology Academy."
- *Gargantuan Garbler* — "lurkers hate children" (2nd Ed subtitle).
- *Gnat* / *Swarm of Gnats* — "In Darkest Lugubria, gnat swats you!"; Millicent Holcroft to General J.G. Blargington III: "Yes, gnats! Vicious, nasty gnats with big, pointy stingers!…"
- *Nearby Serpent*, *Servile Centipede* ("your slithering servant"), *Rummaging Millipede* ("I should have sent the centipede."), *Sneaking Spitspider*, *Spastic Tentacles* ("For the last time, Myrtle! Lock the bedroom door when we have company over!").
- *Lugubrian Poacher* is a lurker.

A bird in a treasure chest."), *Crow With Fists* ("It's a crow. / With fists."), *Fwing Snipe* (subtitle "swallowed the finger, kept the ring"), *Fwing!* ("fly, little brother! bring us back a shiny!"), *Gassy Gastornis* ("tastes like chicken" / "smells like a three day old corpse stuffed with garlic and bear poop"), *Swollen Ostrich*, *Ferrous Ferret*.
