# Elves ("31f" / "31v35")

The gearsmith people. Cards write their name in leetspeak: **31f** (singular; the card
subtype, 49 Gearsmith cards plus a few others) and **31v35** / **31v15h** (plural/adjective).
The card *I Got the Gear!* uses the plain word: "Elves can construct any tool you need.
However, their unintelligible language makes special orders particularly difficult." *Devastating
Mockery* (Open Beta) says "the elves of Bay 13". Leetspeak itself is the voice of most
Gearsmith card subtitles and some flavor text.

---

## Culture
- **Voidal engineering tradition** — *Micromajig* (Ambassador Mode): "the culture of invention in Acrolos is deeply seated in the traditions of voidal engineering practiced by the 31v35, who comprise a very significant portion of the community there. Since the first 31f expatriates settled in Turncrank Gorge in the days of old Marmothoa, they have practiced a small but strict, almost religious, set of tenets governing their art. One of these tenets is the stalwart abhorrence of mass production."
- **Sex-segregated homelands/behaviour**: *Micromajig* footnote — "it is nearly impossible to have a mature conversation with a male 31f, and nearly all the female 31v35 have sequestered themselves on N1d14, which we call Nidia." *53cur17y 5y573m* — "female 31v35 usually don't leave their homeland Nidia." *Thabbashite Convert* — (subtitle) "male 31v35 often leave home" / "Sometimes, they never return."
- **Food**: *B14nd P3pp3r* — "mild by 31v15h taste"; the pepper cards (*H07 P3pp3r*, *P3pp3r 0f 1n54n17y*).
- **Lifespan hints**: *Research Assistant* — "F14xu5 didn't spend 300 years in runecrafting school to bring snacks to some punk one fifth his age." *Research Intern* (Seed Saga subtitle) — "i'm hoping to finish my dissertation in about eighty years". *Beefy Researcher* — "6732 years young".
- **Work**: research assistants/interns, mud whisperers, mechanics, tinkers, runic tattoo artists (*Runic Tattoo Artist*), smelders (*Warmachine Smelder*), conveyers (*Abused 31f Conveyer*), "Nut Punchers" who ride point on mechanical caravans (*Lunk Nut*).
- **Guardforce / military**: *Guardforce Alpha Prime 31f*, *Guardforce Alpha Prime H4x0r*, *R3p347 0ff3nd3r*; *Customized 31f* (Imperial Army experiments); *6r4wny 60dy9u4rd5* (Warlord, `31f, mercenary`: "Please allow me to introduce my associates, Max Deltoid and Chunk McSlabmeats.").
- **Defection**: *Thabbashite Convert* (`31f, fighter`).
- **A resister**: *0v3rw0rk3d 31f* — "One 31f alone at the barricade confronted armored carmajigs, while others fled. He stood for freedom for us all, but few care now if he's jailed or dead."
- **Hostility to mocking/ridicule**: *Devastating Mockery*; *Unrelenting Ridicule* ("They'll get tired of it in a few hundred years or so...").
