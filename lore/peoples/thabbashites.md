# Thabbashites

The desert tribes who worship Living Gods. They appear in every trade (33 distinct card names containing
"Thabbash"/"Thabbashite") and the [V] threshold icon appears on the Universal cards. Religion details: `../world/religion-and-gods.md`.

---

## Core statement
*The Primitive Tribes of Thabbash* (Holy Heist faction card): "In the desert outside of Marduun, the warring Thabbash tribes worship their Living Gods. Incapable of communication and devoid of intelligence, The Living Gods melt people and animals, cause spontaneous combustion, and transform their followers into monsters. Their follower's only hope for survival will be the use of their own volition..."

## Society
- **Mystery to others**: *Chosen Reaper of Thabbash* — "Thabbashite society is largely a mystery to the peoples of Luridia, but one thing is certain: if you see a Reaper, run."
- **Tribes named on cards**: **Muelon** (*Thabbashite Communicant*: "The women of the Muelon tribe believe that heart's blood, properly prepared and served, satiates the living gods."; *Volatile Communion*: "muelon tribeswomen: heartbreakers, the lot of 'em"), **Geroam** (*Thabbashite Penumbra*: "Geroam tribeswomen prefer poison darts to blowguns. They're just as effective as their male counterparts."; *Thabbashite Dartist*: "Blow guns are a favored weapon among the tribe of Geroam. One shot. One kill. No exceptions."), **Doarden** (*Thabbashite Deacon*: "The clergy of the Doarden tribes…"; *Thabbashite Seer*: "The seers of the Doarden tribes lose their eyes at an early age, but gain a new kind of sight in exchange."; *Doarden Flee Market*).
- **Warlike**: "warring Thabbash tribes" (above); *Thabbashite Warmonger*, *Gor-blach the Endowed* ("thabbashite champion"), *Thabbashite Armorer* ("…and the maker is honored to test them first."), *Thabbashite Host*, *Thabbashite Glutton* ("unstoppable calories of doom"), *Thabbashite Bowlegger* ("Riding beaver into glory!"), *Warbeast of Thabbash*, *Guardbeast of Thabbash*.

Don't be fooled by their tentacle monster worship - not every Thabbashite is unlearned." *Thabbashite Chemist* ("the living gods call for potent magics"). *Thabbashite Financier* ("The value of gold transcends culture."). *Akashic Coin* (fake currency via the void).
- **Prisoners/slaves**: *Direstock Cuffs* (subtitle) — "see also: thabbashite slaves".
