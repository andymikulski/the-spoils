# Sarcophyles

A Rogue-trade people (card subtype `sarcophyle`). The cards never use the word "vampire", but
they associate sarcophyles with blood, near-immortality, "converting", "essence", and a
"plague". Everything below is quoted; no equivalence to any other creature type is asserted.

---

## Statements
- *Hyperphagic Sarcophyle* (subtitle "bottomless glutton of blood") — "The suave sarcophyle we know in society is a myth; if our young people really understood what these monsters become, if they could see the wake of tragedy and despair, they would never seek to convert." — Ernest Arndell [Open Beta and 2nd Ed: "Amdell"], *A Cancer in our Midst: On Sarcophelia and the Coming Plague*.
- *Sarcophyle Matriculation* (subtitle "some choices can never be undone") — "Those seeking cultured notoriety and a bohemian lifestyle beg Sarcophyle lords to share their essence."
- *Forced Recruitment* (1st Ed subtitle "busty sarcophyles...my only weakness!"; 2nd Ed subtitle "putting the 'sensual' back in 'non-consensual'") — "Bored with her pallid harem, the sarcophyle mistress is always hungry for handsome new playthings."
- *Sickened Sarcophyle* (subtitle "nearly immortal invalid") — "While sarcophyles are resilient against most ailments, they are strangely vulnerable to a few, such as anemia."
- *Unchained Sarcophyle* (subtitle "doom bringer") — "Mutations, albeit uncommon, are known to exist among the Sarcophyle classes. First the mind rots; then the body revolts."
- *Torchlight Sarcophyle* (subtitle "it's death o'clock") — "I'm going to give you the choice I never had."
- *Bipolar Sarcophyle* (subtitle) — "cast into the gloomy depths of eternal cheer"
- *Hasty Sarcophyle* — "Man, that is one hasty sarcophyle. Whoosh." (subtitle "he's hasty. and a sarcophyle.")
- *Sneaky Sarco* (card subtypes `hedonist, sarcophyle`; subtitle) — "such a beautiful way to die"
- *Smear Campaign* — "Despite his moderate populist stance on foreign policy and the sarcophyle problem, Senator Tysonburg's penchant for babymeat proved too much for voters to stomach."
- "Sarcophyle classes", "Sarcophyle lords", "sarcophyle mistress" — plural social tiers are implied by *Unchained Sarcophyle*, *Sarcophyle Matriculation* and *Forced Recruitment*.
- "Pallid harem" — *Forced Recruitment*.

## Named sarcophyles (card subtype `sarcophyle`)
- **Vander Malagut** — subtitle "seething hatred, seductive wit". (Unique.)
- **Cicibeo, The Implicator** — subtitle "it's the implication"; card subtypes `grifter, cicibeo, sarcophyle`. (A different card, *Cicibeo the Swift*, is a `grifter, cicibeo`: "Excellent dancer, better pickpocket." The cards do not say whether it is the same person.)

## Placement
- All sarcophyle character cards are Rogue-trade. No card ties sarcophyles to a specific region.
