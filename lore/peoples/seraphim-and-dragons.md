# Seraphim and Dragons

Card subtype `dragon` has 66 cards (53 of them Warlord); `seraphim` has 15 (each also tagged
`dragon`). The cards use "dragon" broadly: barduse, basks, shrievers, sarume, nilpha and the
named seraphim all carry the subtype. They are mounts, war-beasts, officers and (for the
named seraphim) individual characters.

---

- *Breath of the Barduse* (subtitle) — "indiscriminate carnage"

- *Invade!* — "Adriel and the newly born Barduse". Barduse is both a creature type and a name; the cards use it in both senses.
