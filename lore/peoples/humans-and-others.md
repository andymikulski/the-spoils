# Humans and Other Peoples

Catch-all for peoples that do not have their own file. The cards almost never name a
character's race; most Warlord soldiers, Rogue thieves and Banker clerks are simply "a man",
"a woman", "he". This file records every explicit statement about humans and about the minor
groups that do appear.

---

## Humans
- Explicit uses of "human": *Puddle of Goo* ("the role of the human digestive system"), *Real Estate Auctioneer* ("a slight human infestation"), *Sleeping Guard* (subtitle "he didn't like being human anyway"), *12 Man Brunch of Doom* (subtitle "very well sir, human head stuffed with tacos"), *Goldmau Sacks Representative* ("wrapped around the face of humanity").
- "Man and beast": *Customized 31f* — "The Imperial Army is known to experiment on all manner of man and beast."
- "The peoples of Luridia": *Chosen Reaper of Thabbash*. Which peoples are meant is never listed.
- *Thabbashite Convert*, *Unfortunate Fortune Hunter*, *Loyal Companion* etc. show characters from one people living among others (see their files).
- No card defines humans' relationship to Mau, elves or the other peoples.

## Lingamites
- Faction in the war: *Storm the Gates* — "Thabbashites versus Adriel and the newly formed Barduse versus Lingamites, Swords and the Seraphim."
- *Lingamite Torture Monk* (Arcanist `priest`; Seed Saga subtitle) — "we call this 'enhanced interrogation'".
- *Lingamite Descendant* (Warlord `soldier`) — "Yes, his grandfather was Gideon. His father was deaf and when all of Gideon's other children were turned into barduse, he remained unchanged. Through his sire, Gideon's legacy remains." (subtitle "muh grad fodder wa a lingamite warror"; framing line: "I don't care about your family tree, but you ARE one of our best deaf soldiers.")

## Swords of Great Justice, Blades of Questionable Morality, Demon Knights
Card-subtype groupings of named, unique characters rather than peoples; see `../characters/marduun-era.md` and `../characters/unlikely-heroes.md`.

## Pluck (the subtype)
- Subtype `pluck` (Rogue, plus the Banker *Mr. Fetch*): *Pluck*, *The Billionaire*, *Cheats McGuillicutty*, *Cheats, the Dirty Rotten*, *Mr. Fetch*, *Bottlebeard Brandyshanks*, *Abram Ka-darbo Alakazin*, etc.
- *Chum, the Ugliest Sarume* — "A Pluck's best friend."
- *Difference of Opinion* (subtitle) — "a pluck and a chum walk onto a battlefield"
- *Zenobia's Intervention* — "'Great Justice?' Pluck said." (Pluck is a named speaker.)

## Merlions
- *Avuncular Merlion* (Banker; two printings) — "rescue pirates can, la" / "But keep some for uncle, la."

## Changeling
- *Soulsearcher* (Universal character, Ungodly Mess) — card subtype `changeling`; no flavor.

## Pacifists
- *Conscientious Objector* — "Destroying his home was a BIG mistake." / "These. Are. My. FLOWERS!!!!" ; *Enlightened Mau* (`mau, pacifist`).

## Clowns, performers, hamsters
- `clowns`: *Party Clowns*; `clown`: *Dweedle Tee* ("Contrariwise, if it was so, it might be…" ⚠).
- Hamsters: *The Abominable Hamster* (monstrosity) and *Claywork Compendium* ("written by really smart hamsters").

## Giants and small folk
- "Giants": *Legacy Warmachine* ("Those middle blades are for fighting giants. Granted there aren't any, but you never know."), *Banana Peel* ("surprisingly effective against giants too!"), *Low-hanging Clothesline*.
- "Midgets": *Midget Mine* ("only 50% effective on non-midgets"), *Duelist of Surfeit* ("Midget Glaive?") ⚠ — term as printed; no people is defined.
- *Dwarvish Grimalkin* (Mau): "judge me by my size, do you?"
