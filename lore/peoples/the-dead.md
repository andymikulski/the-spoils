# Ghosts, Necromorphs and the Dead

Banker-trade cards dominate: the card subtypes `ghost` (7 cards) and `necromorph` (7 cards).
Death in the catalog is an *economy* (debt, resurrection services, estates), not a mystery.

## How death is handled in-world
- **Becoming a necromorph is a known outcome**: *Resurrection Technician* — "My God! That's the chairman of Empire Impoverishments! Doesn't he realize he'll return as a necromorph?" / "It's what the will says. Pull the lever, Max."
- **A necromorph can be poached**: *Executive Poaching* — "He's putting for eagle...this is for the entire tournament...Oh! He was stolen by a necromorph! That's got to be disappointing!"
- **Corpses as property**: *Marduun Baitings and Loan* — "Just sign here where it says we get to turn your corpse into a mindless zombie when you die." *Montgomery Blatherscythe* — "every corpse has its price". *Recycling Scheme Facility* — "reduce, reuse, reanimate" with a "mostly dead" speech. *Resurrection Emporium* (location). *Grandiose Resurrector* (Mau). *Postmortem Debenture* (funeral debt). *Crematory Cache*.
- **Other revival cards**: *Dark Awakening* ("welcome back to the land of the living"), *Revivatate* ("almost actually a word"), *Shocking Reappearance* ("Moving on to the subject of our former chairman's unfortunate demise...Great Scott! Our former chairman!"), *Miraculous Regeneration* ("remember when you said you could sleep when you're dead?" — "You were wrong."), *Necromantic Healer* ("bringing life to some and less life to others"), *Aroused Stiffbone* (Warlord dragon; "what is dead may never die" / "But rises again, harder and stronger.").
- **Cults**: *Padamose Cultists* — "worshipping the undead" (subtitle).
- **Death-related art/sport**: *Fallen Quarter-Finalist* (above).
